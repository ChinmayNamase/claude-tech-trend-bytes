# Before you press Publish (2 minutes)

1. **Run the gate:** `./8-blog-system/check-draft.sh 2-blog-posts/drafts/<file>.md` — no must-fix items.
2. **Read the opening three paragraphs aloud.** Do they sound like you?
3. **Check every price and date** against the linked source.
4. **Add the featured image** — at least 1,200px wide, and write what it shows in the alt text box.
5. **Check the Google preview** in Yoast: title under 60 characters, description under 156.
6. **Publish**, then tell Claude so it can ask Google to index it.
